Out of the Vacuum, Into the Ether

Motivation

Physics should make sense. The science communicatiors who hype up misunderstandings have done a lot of damage to the field. It didn’t start with quantum mechanics, but this is not a history lesson.

Assumptions

Bodies are necessary to describe physics. Fields are descriptions of forces but they must be attached to bodies. Potentials should be preferred over fields, not only because of the A-B effect or gauge invariance, which show that fields are definitely not primary, but also because it is natural to ask “what causes this potential?”.

The answer should be “the properties of physical bodies moving, deforming, interacting”

The Reframe

Previous ether theories thought there were other properties to the ether. The ether was ruled out before special relativity and before most modern tech was developed using electromagnetic waves. The vacuum can be reconceptualized as the ether without changing contemporary physics. This is powerful because it does not require overturning everything but can lead to new questions.

First we start with an important question - how does light travel? The conventional answer is “through the vacuum”, i.e. through “nothingness”. That does not make sense, but fortunately we can reframe our way out of this.

The ether has two material properties

  • electric permittivity
  • magnetic permeability

These determine the speed of light:

\[ c = \frac{1}{ \sqrt{ \mu \epsilon }} \]

There is also a notion of “vacuum impedance”. This is a strange idea…how does “nothing” have impedance and material parameters? Of course, a medium can have impedance, so this makes perfect sense if we call it ether instead of vacuum.

The units of the material represent inductance and capacitance per meter. The use of “impedance” can be misleading because it is a general notion of resistance. Complex impedance has a real and imaginary component. Resistors contribute to the real part, inductors and capacitors to the imaginary part
